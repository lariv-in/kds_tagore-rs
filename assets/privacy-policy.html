Privacy Policy & Account Deletion

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Last updated: September 27, 2026

KDS and Tagore Pvt. Ltd. ("KDS Tagore," "we," "our," or "us") operates the KDS Tagore mobile application and progressive web app (the "App"), together with our public website and connected business software (collectively, the "Services"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. It applies specifically to the KDS Tagore App and related Services.

1. Who We Are

KDS and Tagore Pvt. Ltd. provides manufacturing operations software for quotations, work orders, scheduling, finance, CRM, HR, and related business workflows. If you have questions about this policy, contact us using the details in Section 12.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Services, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account and authentication data: name, email address, password (stored in hashed form), role and permission settings, login timestamps, and session or authentication tokens needed to keep you signed in.

  • Contact and business profile data: phone numbers, company names, billing and shipping details, customer and supplier records, CRM contacts, and other information you or your organization enter into the Services.

  • Operational and financial data: quotations, work orders, machine schedules, invoices, taxes, payments, inventory, HR records, tasks, forms, and other business records created or uploaded through the App.

  • Files and content you upload: CAD drawings, PDFs, images, spreadsheets, and other documents stored in the Services for production, quoting, or record-keeping.

  • Communications: messages you send to us for support, password-reset or one-time passcode (OTP) emails, and other correspondence related to your account or use of the Services.

  • Technical and device data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers available to a web or mobile app, app version, pages or screens viewed, timestamps, diagnostic logs, and similar usage information collected automatically when you access the Services.

  • Local storage and cookies: essential cookies, authentication cookies, service-worker cache entries, and browser or app storage needed for sign-in, security, and offline functionality.

  • Collaboration and assistant features: if you use integrated meeting, messaging, or AI-assistant features, we may process the content, prompts, and metadata needed to provide those features.

We do not require you to provide sensitive categories of data unless your organization chooses to store them for legitimate business purposes. If you upload such data, you are responsible for ensuring you have the right to do so.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • create and manage user accounts and authenticate access to the App;

  • provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Services;

  • process quotations, work orders, scheduling, finance, CRM, HR, and other business workflows you request;

  • store, display, and generate documents or exports you request;

  • send service-related communications such as security alerts, OTP messages, and support responses;

  • monitor performance, troubleshoot errors, prevent fraud or abuse, and protect the security of the Services;

  • comply with law, enforce our terms, and respond to lawful requests; and

  • fulfill any other purpose disclosed to you at the time of collection, with your consent where required.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your business records to train general-purpose AI models for unrelated third parties.

4. How We Share Information

We share information only as described below:

  • Service providers: trusted vendors that host infrastructure, provide email delivery, store backups, or otherwise help us operate the Services. These providers may access information only to perform services for us and must protect it under contractual obligations.

  • Your organization: if you access the App through an employer or customer account, administrators authorized by that organization may access information associated with that account.

  • Integrated third-party services: if your organization connects external tools (for example, email, AI, or communication providers), data may be shared with those services as directed by your organization's configuration.

  • Legal and safety reasons: when required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect rights, safety, or security.

  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate notice where required by law.

We do not share personal information with advertisers for cross-app tracking.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal and business information for as long as needed to provide the Services, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods may vary based on the type of data and your organization's settings. When information is no longer required, we delete or anonymize it unless law requires longer retention.

6. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including HTTPS/TLS encryption for data in transit and industry-standard practices for access control and password hashing. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;

  • request correction of inaccurate information;

  • request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal exceptions;

  • object to or restrict certain processing;

  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and

  • receive a copy of certain information in a portable format.

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@kdstagore.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding. If your account is managed by an organization, some requests may need to be submitted through that organization's administrator.

8. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your KDS Tagore account and associated personal data at any time. Account deletion is available both in the App workflow below and through this publicly accessible page, as required by Google Play.

To request account deletion:

  1. Email support@kdstagore.com from the address linked to your account.

  2. Use the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

  3. Include your full name, organization name (if applicable), and the email address used to sign in.

After we verify your request, we will delete or anonymize personal data associated with your account, except where retention is required by law, for legitimate security purposes, or to complete an open business obligation (for example, tax or invoice records your organization is required to keep). We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days and will confirm by email when processing is finished.

9. Children's Privacy

The KDS Tagore App is a business application and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use essential cookies, local storage, and service-worker caching to keep you signed in, protect the Services, and support core App functionality. We do not use these technologies for third-party advertising or cross-site tracking. You can control cookies through your browser settings, but disabling essential cookies may prevent parts of the App from working.

11. International Users

The Services may be accessed worldwide. If you use the App from outside India, your information may be processed in India or in other countries where we or our service providers operate. We take steps designed to protect information in accordance with this policy and applicable law.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If changes are material, we may also provide additional notice (for example, by email or in-app notification). Your continued use of the Services after an update becomes effective means you accept the revised policy.
